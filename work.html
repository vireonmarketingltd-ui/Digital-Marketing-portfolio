Ebook Business — Multi-Country Meta Ads (8 Markets)

As part of the 9bizub role, managed Meta Ads for a stakeholder's separate Kindle and Selar ebook business — running campaigns simultaneously across 8 countries. The challenge: each market had different buying behaviour, price sensitivity, and creative resonance. Required a corrected optimisation strategy after inheriting underperforming campaigns.

Key Actions

Inherited underperforming campaigns and immediately audited the optimisation event structure
Shifted to a higher-volume optimisation event to build pixel signal — critical step before scaling to purchase-based optimisation
Managed simultaneous campaigns across 8 countries with localised creative and targeting adjustments
Balanced budget allocation across markets based on weekly performance data
Provided detailed performance reports to the stakeholder with strategic recommendations
